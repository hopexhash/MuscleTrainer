MuscleTrainer Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Your data stays on your iPhone

MuscleTrainer stores everything it knows — your profile, saved workouts, completed sessions, favorites and preferences — locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate accounts, we do not run analytics, and we do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app contains no third-party SDKs and no advertising.

Exercise videos

The app streams exercise demonstration videos from the MuscleTrainer video library (or from a custom server if you configure one in Profile). These requests fetch only the video list and the video files themselves — they carry no account, no identifiers, and nothing about you or your training.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. There is nothing to delete on our side, because nothing ever leaves your device.

Health & safety

MuscleTrainer provides general fitness information, not medical advice. Consult a physician before starting a new exercise program, stop if you feel pain or discomfort, and train within your abilities. You are responsible for exercising safely.

Children

MuscleTrainer is not directed at children under 13.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version is published at this URL with a new effective date. Questions: see the support page.
