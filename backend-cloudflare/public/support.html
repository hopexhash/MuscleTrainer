MuscleTrainer Support

Getting started

Open the Body tab and tap any muscle to see its exercises, or let the AI Coach build a session for you. Workouts, history and progress all live on your device — no account needed.

Common questions

Where is my data? Everything is stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Exercise videos don't play. Check Profile → Exercise videos: the server URL must be reachable from your phone, and each video is uploaded per exercise on the server's admin page.

How do I reset the app? Delete and reinstall — the exercise catalog and example workouts are re-seeded on first launch.
